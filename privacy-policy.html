Privacy Policy

Football Club 2026

Effective date: October 24, 2026
Package: com.agenticfootball.game
Developer: Mehedi13Wp

This policy describes information handling in Football Club 2026, including the offline testing release version 0.14.4.

Information collection and sharing

The current game operates offline. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information through the game. It has no account registration, sign-in, advertising, analytics, in-app purchases or connection to a developer-operated server.

Local game information

Club details, settings, gameplay progress and earned in-game coins are stored locally on your device to provide game functionality and retain progress. The game does not upload or automatically synchronise this information to a developer server or transfer it to another device.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device until you reset or remove it. You can delete it using Android Settings to clear the app's storage or by uninstalling the app. This may permanently remove your progress. The developer does not hold a server copy of your game progress.

Permissions

The current release does not request Android permissions to access contacts, camera, microphone, location or shared photos and files.

Children's privacy

The current game does not collect personal information from players, including children, and has no advertising or account registration.

Privacy questions and support

You can contact the developer by opening a privacy or support issue in the public policy repository: Contact Mehedi13Wp. A GitHub account is required to submit an issue. Issues are public: do not include passwords, private identifiers or other sensitive information. Information you voluntarily post is used to respond to your request and investigate reported problems. GitHub handles account and website information under its own privacy statement.

Google Play and this policy website

Google Play may process download, distribution and diagnostic information under Google's own policies. This is separate from the game's offline processing. This policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process visitor information, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. No analytics, advertising scripts or third-party fonts are added to this page.

Security

Game progress is stored on your device. Protect access to your device to help protect this information.

Policy updates

If features or information-handling practices change, this policy will be updated with a revised effective date.
